About

I am an R&D Associate Staff Member in the Building Equipment Group at Oak Ridge National Laboratory. My work focuses on the design, modeling, and experimental evaluation of HVAC and heat pump technologies, next-generation refrigerants, and fault detection and diagnostics.

I combine laboratory testing, system modeling, and field demonstrations to advance energy-efficient heating, cooling, and thermal storage technologies. My background also includes HVAC engineering design and building energy audits.

Research Interests

  • Design, modeling, and testing of HVAC systems
  • HVAC fault detection and diagnostics
  • Virtual calibration, virtual sensing, and soft repair
  • Heat pump technologies
  • Flow boiling and condensation
  • Thermoelectric cooling and heating
  • Thermal energy storage

Selected Research Projects

Emerging Refrigerant Thermal-Hydraulic Characterization

Principal Investigator · 2026–present

Improve flow boiling facilities and develop flow condensation testing to characterize heat transfer and pressure drop of next-generation refrigerants.

Two-Phase Heat Transfer and Pressure Drop Characterization

Lead researcher; Principal Investigator since 2026 · 2024–present

Characterize next-generation refrigerants and support implementation in heat exchanger and system models, including A3 refrigerant testing.

Cold Climate Integrated Heat Pump

Lead researcher; Co-Principal Investigator since 2026 · 2024–present

Develop high-efficiency, multi-stage air-source integrated heat pumps through laboratory prototyping and field studies.

Next-Generation Low-Cost Direct-Expansion Heat Pumps

Co-Principal Investigator · 2026–present

Develop packaged propane secondary heat pump prototypes and assess a CO₂ refrigerant mixture heat pump concept.

AI-Based Refrigerant Leakage Detection

Co-Principal Investigator · 2025–present

Develop virtual refrigerant charge sensors for air conditioners and heat pumps using next-generation refrigerants.

Cold Climate Heat Pump with Thermoelectric Integration

Lead researcher · 2023–present

Design, fabricate, and test vapor-compression heat pumps cascaded with thermoelectric heat pumps, including field studies.

Compressor is a Sensor

Participant; Lead researcher since 2026 · 2024–present

Investigate heat pump fault behavior and develop fault detection and diagnosis methods in collaboration with Copeland.

Residential Simultaneous Dual-Fuel HVAC

Lead researcher · 2026–present

Develop cold-climate residential HVAC combining vapor compression with a refrigerant gas boiler.
